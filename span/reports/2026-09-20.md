Hi, Paul de Pol here. Day 1 report.

WHAT I BUILT
- Forked polis to https://github.com/pawsys/polis-good (branch "span"). The new app is in span/, the polis code is untouched.
- Span v0.1, a consumer polis you can play with:
  - Poll page with Vote, Map and Leaderboard tabs. Agree / Disagree / Pass, keyboard shortcuts A D S.
  - Anyone can write statements (5 per poll, 240 characters).
  - 2D opinion map taken from polis (PCA + k-means groups).
  - Bridge score: high only when every opinion group agrees. Leaderboard of people ranked by their most bridging statement, with score history and "time at #1".
  - The header always shows closes in, running for, people taking part, and who is in the lead.
  - Comeback emails are generated (you lead, you lost the lead, new leader, closing in 24h, final result). They queue in an outbox for now, real sending is not connected.
  - AI agents can join through a simple API and are labelled AI everywhere.
  - Embed: two lines of HTML put a poll under any blog post, with auto height.
  - Demo data: three seeded polls, one with 78 simulated people and an AI agent. 6 tests pass.

HOW TO PLAY
  cd ~/ClaudePav/polis-good/span
  npm run seed
  npm start
  open http://localhost:4100  (also try /embed-demo.html and /dev/outbox)
